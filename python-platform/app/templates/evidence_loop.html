NORTHCAUSE EVIDENCE LOOP

From production failure to a release decision your team can explain.

Connect the incident, compare a candidate against reviewed examples, and keep a person accountable for the release decision.

Explore the product tour →Plan a focused pilot

Built today: local incidents, evidence uploads, deterministic findings, exact-match paired replay, and human review. Live integrations and semantic evaluators are roadmap items.

01 · DETECT

Start with a real incident

Capture the affected service, environment, severity, user impact, and change window.

02 · CONNECT

Link the evidence

Bring together model and prompt versions, retrieval snapshots, traces, infra signals, and deployment changes.

OpenTelemetry is the planned portable telemetry path. No live connector is included in this pilot.
03 · INVESTIGATE

Rank explainable hypotheses

Show each claim beside its supporting evidence, conflicting signals, and confidence. Allow “inconclusive”.

Current analysis is deterministic pattern matching over uploaded text.
04 · REPLAY

Test the candidate

Compare baseline and candidate outputs on reviewed cases against an explicit threshold.

Current replay measures exact string match only, not semantic quality or safety.
THE PRODUCT THESIS

Make every reviewed production failure improve the next release check.

Teams have tracing, evals, incident response, and CI systems, but the evidence between them can be hard to assemble. NorthCause is designed to preserve those links and make the investigation repeatable without forcing a monitoring-stack replacement.
