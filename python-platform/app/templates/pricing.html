Questions about pricing

What does a pilot include?

A short discovery defines one or more workflows, approved evidence sources, success criteria, and deployment requirements. Scope and price are agreed before work begins.

Can we use our own model provider?

Optional external model use depends on deployment configuration, provider terms, and an agreed data-processing arrangement. It is disabled by default.

Are listed integrations available today?

Availability depends on the current pilot build. The team will confirm each required connector and its access model during scoping.
