Pricing principles

What drives the future price?

Active services, replay volume, retention, private deployment, support, and integration scope. Avoid charging separately for every seat and every surrounding trace without showing the cost driver.

Can we use our own model provider?

The present pilot makes no external model calls. Future provider support must be optional, configurable, and covered by an explicit data-processing decision.

Are these capabilities available now?

No. The current local build includes incident records, manual text evidence, deterministic findings, human-reviewed drafts, and exact-match replay. The other packaging items are proposals.
