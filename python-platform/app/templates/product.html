Infrastructure context

Relate application symptoms to the environment where the model ran, including GPU, Linux, Kubernetes, and service evidence.

  • Development, staging, and production labels
  • GPU and cluster evidence
  • Service and release context

Human review and verification

Keep remediation advisory. Record who reviewed a proposed next step, why they decided, and what follow-up evidence supports the result.

  • Reviewable remediation drafts
  • Decision rationale recorded
  • Follow-up evidence attached
